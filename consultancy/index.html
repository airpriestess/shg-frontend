The actual problem

It was never that you couldn't make content.

So I write your taste down as rules anything can follow — your team, your freelancers, your tools — and then I build what enforces them.

Straight answers

Questions people actually ask.

Do I need to already have a brand?

Yes. I enforce an identity, I don't invent one from nothing. If you have colours, type and a voice you recognise, that's enough to work with.

Do you run our content for us?

No. I build the system and hand it over — your team runs it. If you want someone to produce your posts every week, I'll point you to someone who does that.

What if the output still isn't right?

The System includes a revision round, and a tool that produces something you wouldn't publish isn't finished. That's the standard I hold my own work to.

Can my team use this without me?

That's the whole point. Everything goes to your team — not to a login you rent from me.

How fast can you start?

Usually the week after the call. I take a small number at a time so each one gets finished properly.
